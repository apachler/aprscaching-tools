# CW encoder

The CW encoder turns text into Morse code, written as dots and dashes. It is the send side of the
[CW decoder](digimode-decoders.md), for operators who practise or prepare a CW message.

## Use it

| Command | Answer |
|---|---|
| `/cw SOS` | `... --- ...` |
| `/cw` | `Usage: /cw <text>` |

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `command` | Registers `/cw` |
